# Ask a fresh reader

Use this after you have audited a brief yourself. Open a **new conversation**, with no history,
and paste the brief and nothing else. The new conversation sees only the page, as the person (or
AI) doing the work will. Whatever it stumbles on, they would have had to guess at.

A colleague who was not in the meeting works too.

```
I am about to hand the brief below to someone who will do the work without being able to ask me
any questions. Read only what is written here.

List every point where they would have to guess: a missing detail, a word that could mean two
things, a step that assumes something not written down, or two parts that disagree.

For each one, say what they would probably guess, and what I should add to the brief so they do
not have to.

The brief:
<paste the brief here>
```
