# Audit the brief

Use this before you hand a long task to an AI, or to a person: paste your plan or brief below the
prompt, in the same conversation where you wrote it.

```
Here is a plan for a task. Before anyone starts work on it, audit it.

Assume it contains at least two mistakes. For each mistake you find:
- quote the part of the plan it is in,
- say what would go wrong if someone followed the plan exactly as written,
- suggest a fix.

Look in particular for: steps that contradict each other; numbers, limits or counts that are
wrong, off by one, or not stated; places where the person doing the work would have to guess;
and claims that nothing in the plan checks.

If you find fewer than two mistakes after a careful search, say so, and say what you checked.

The plan:
<paste the plan here>
```

Fix what it finds, then give the corrected plan to a fresh reader:
[fresh-reader.md](fresh-reader.md).
